## 🎮 How the Auto Farm System Works

Understanding the internal loop of the **Auto Farm Adopt Me Script Pastebin** system helps you configure it more effectively. The script operates in three parallel threads that run without interfering with each other.

**Thread 1 — Candy Collection Loop:**
The **Auto Farm Candy** thread scans the game world for active Candy sources, moves the character to each one in sequence, and triggers the collection action. It then waits for the respawn timer and repeats. This loop alone can collect several thousand Candy per hour depending on server conditions.

**Thread 2 — Pet Management Loop:**
The **Auto Farm Pet Adopt Me Script** and **Auto Hatch Eggs Adopt Me Script** threads work together. Eggs are hatched in priority order based on your config, and freshly hatched pets are immediately enrolled in the **Age Up Potion Adopt Me Script** feeding queue.

**Thread 3 — Trade & Economy Loop:**
When **Auto Trade Adopt Me Script** is enabled, this thread monitors incoming trade requests, evaluates them against your `min_trade_value` config setting, and either triggers **Auto Accept Adopt Me Script** approval or queues a counter-offer. The **Force Accept Adopt Me Script** flag overrides all checks and accepts everything above zero value.

## 🛠️ Troubleshooting Common Issues

❌ **Script GUI does not appear after Execute**
The in-game overlay failed to initialize, usually because injection happened before the game finished loading.
**Solution:** Detach your executor, wait until you can see your character moving freely in Adopt Me, then re-attach and execute again.

---

❌ **Auto Farm Candy stops collecting after a few minutes**
The collection loop hit a navigation dead-end or the server repositioned a Candy source node.
**Solution:** Open `config.lua` and set `debug_log = true`, re-run, and check the console output for the exact loop step where it stalled. Usually a server reconnect resolves it.

---

❌ **Auto Accept Adopt Me Script is accepting trades I didn't want**
Your `min_trade_value` is set too low or **Force Accept Adopt Me Script** is enabled unintentionally.
**Solution:** Open `config.lua`, raise `min_trade_value` to a safer number, and confirm `force_accept = false` is set.

---

❌ **Auto Hatch Eggs Adopt Me Script skips some eggs in inventory**
Stack size detection glitch when the inventory contains mixed egg types.
**Solution:** Sort your inventory so all eggs of the same type are grouped before running the hatch loop. This is a known edge case being addressed in the next patch.

---

❌ **Buy Any Egg not triggering on shop restock**
The egg name string in `buy_egg_type` does not match the exact in-game egg name.
**Solution:** Open the in-game egg shop, copy the exact egg name including capitalization, and paste it into `buy_egg_type` in your `config.lua`. Example: `"Golden Egg"` not `"golden egg"`.

---

❌ **Executor crashes on Android when All Pets panel is open**
Running the **All Pets Adopt Me Script** panel on low-RAM devices causes memory overflow.
**Solution:** Close all background apps, restart the executor, and avoid opening the All Pets panel simultaneously with **Auto Farm Pet Adopt Me Script**.
